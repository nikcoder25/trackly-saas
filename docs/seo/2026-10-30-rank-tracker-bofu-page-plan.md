Date: 2026-10-30. Data: DataForSEO (US, Google). Livesov.com today: 60 referring domains, mostly nofollow, DataForSEO rank 81 (roughly DA 10 to 15).

## What the product can honestly claim

From `src/lib/rank-tracking/*`:

- Google organic position tracking, top 30 or top 100
- City level locations (bundled city gazetteer), desktop and mobile
- Weekly checks, daily on Agency plan and up
- White label reports: CSV, HTML, PDF (with graphs), agency name, logo, color, custom domain
- Clients and keyword groups, Slack alerts on rank changes
- Serpfox CSV import (any rank tracker CSV shaped like it)
- Visibility score (CTR weighted) and AI share of voice next to Google rank

Do NOT claim: Google Maps geo-grid, local pack tracking, Bing, YouTube, public rank API.
